Jobs

A job is a piece of work at one of your locations. This guide follows a job from the moment it's raised to the moment it's closed: who can do each step, who's told, and how the job's status moves along.

1. Job statuses at a glance

You never set a job's status yourself. MyFmHub moves it on as each step is done. Normally a job goes New, Unassigned, In Progress, Completed and then Closed, with extra steps in between when you ask for them. How a job gets to In Progress shows which.

StatusWhat it meansWhat moves it on
NewJust raised, waiting for someone to look at it.Approve (to Unassigned) or Reject (to Rejected).
RejectedTurned down when it was new, with a reason. It's finished.Reopen (back to New), for jobs that aren't planned.
UnassignedApproved, waiting to be given to someone.Assign.
Pending AcceptanceGiven to a supplier or a person who needs to accept it first.Accept (to its next step) or Reject (back to Unassigned).
Pending SchedulingWaiting for the assignee to choose the date they'll do the work.Schedule.
ScheduledHas a date that hasn't arrived yet.The date arriving (to In Progress), or Complete if the work's done early.
Pending StartWaiting for the assignee to confirm the work has started.Start.
In ProgressWith the assignee to do. If the job needs start confirmation, they've confirmed it.Complete.
CompletedThe work is done.Close.
ClosedChecked and closed. It's finished.Reopen (back to Completed), for jobs that aren't planned.

Rejected and Closed jobs are never counted as overdue, and they don't appear in the dashboard's compliance figures or the jobs list's "All Open Jobs" status filter.

2. Where jobs come from

  • Reactive jobs are added by hand with Create on the Jobs page, for example when something breaks.
  • Planned jobs are raised automatically from a planned job schedule when it falls due. They follow the same steps as any other job, with a few extra rules because each one sets when the next is due. See Planned Jobs.

Every job starts as New.

3. Approving or rejecting a new job

While a job is New, its page shows Approve and Reject to anyone whose user group has Approve or reject new jobs.

  • Approve moves it to Unassigned, ready to be assigned. To approve several at once, tick them on the Jobs page and choose Actions, then Approve. The box at the top of the table ticks the whole page. Only New jobs are approved: any others you ticked are left as they are, and the message says how many.
  • Reject asks for a reason, then moves it to Rejected. The reason and the time are shown on the job.
  • Rejecting a planned job also moves its schedule on, so the schedule doesn't wait for a job that won't be done. MyFmHub suggests today plus the schedule's frequency as the next due date; you can bring it forward but not later.
  • A rejected job that isn't planned has a Reopen button (with Reopen jobs). It goes back to New, and the reason is cleared, so it can be approved or rejected again.

4. Assigning

An Unassigned job has an Assign button. Choose who'll do the work: one supplier or one person in your organisation. You can also choose any number of helpers, people working with them. The job moves to In Progress, or its first extra step if you've asked for any, and its page shows who it's assigned to and the helpers. The jobs list's Assigned To column shows it too.

How a job gets to In Progress

The assign page has three optional boxes, and they decide the job's steps. If you tick Require Acceptance, Pending Acceptance comes first in every row. If the assignee rejects the job, it goes back to Unassigned.

Require SchedulingRequire Start ConfirmationWhat happens after it's assigned (and accepted, if required)
NoNoIn Progress straight away
NoYesPending Start, then In Progress when they press Start
YesNoPending Scheduling, Scheduled, then In Progress on the date
YesYesPending Scheduling, Pending Start, then In Progress when they press Start

Without start confirmation you don't know exactly when the work began, so the job's Started At is only filled in when someone presses Start.

To give several jobs to the same supplier or person, tick them on the Jobs page and choose Actions, then Assign. Only Unassigned jobs are assigned: any others you ticked are left as they are, and the page and the message say how many. Any helpers you choose go on every job, and the assignee is told about each job separately.

Tick Require Acceptance when assigning if the supplier or person should agree to the job first. The job moves to Pending Acceptance, and the notification or email has an Accept / Reject Job button. Suppliers, and people who can't sign in, don't need to sign in for this: the link in their email opens the page, works for 30 days, and stops working once they've answered or the job has been reassigned. Only the assignee answers, not helpers.

  • Accept moves the job on to its next step.
  • Reject moves it back to Unassigned and removes the assignee and helpers, so it can be assigned again. Whoever assigned it gets a notification under the bell, and its View button opens the job.
  • If a supplier answers another way, for example by phone, open the job and use Accept / Reject for them. That needs Assign jobs, and start and complete them for the assignee, the same as starting a job for them.

Tick Require Start Confirmation if the assignee should confirm when the work begins. The job waits at Pending Start and shows Start. Suppliers, and people who can't sign in, get a Start Job link by email that needs no sign-in and works for a year. It comes with the last email before the work starts: the assignment email, or a separate one once they've accepted or chosen a date. People who can sign in use the Start button on the job's page.

Tick Require Scheduling if the assignee should confirm the date they'll do the work. The job moves to Pending Scheduling as soon as it's assigned, or once it's accepted if it also requires acceptance. The notification has a Schedule Job button, and the email a link that needs no sign-in. The job's page has a Schedule button too. The date can't be in the past or after the job's due date, unless the due date has already passed. Once there's a date the job goes to Pending Start if it needs start confirmation. Otherwise it's Scheduled until the date arrives, then moves to In Progress by itself. A job can be completed early while it's Scheduled.

Until the work starts, the date can be changed with Reschedule on the job's page. On a job without start confirmation that's until it's completed, because it moved to In Progress by itself: a later date puts it back to Scheduled. Suppliers, and people who can't sign in, get a reschedule link in the same email as the Start Job link, or with the Complete Job link if the job doesn't need start confirmation. If the date changes, whoever assigned the job gets a notification under the bell. Reassigning a job keeps its date.

Suppliers, and people who can't sign in, also get a Complete Job link at the point the work can be completed: after they start the job if it needs start confirmation, otherwise after they choose a date or accept it, if those are needed, otherwise in the assignment email. It opens a page asking when the work was done and what was done, and it works for a year. Nobody is told when a job is completed this way: the dashboard's Completed Jobs card shows the jobs to review and close.

Only the supplier or person it's assigned to is told. Helpers aren't, and neither are you when you assign a job to yourself.

  • People who can sign in get a notification under the bell at the top of every page. View opens the job. The bell's See all notifications lists older ones.
  • Suppliers, and people who can't sign in, get an email with the job's details. A supplier with no email address isn't told, so let them know yourself.

5. Starting and completing the work

The person the job is assigned to uses the buttons on the job's page. They don't need any jobs permissions for this, just to be able to sign in. Anyone with Assign jobs, and start and complete them for the assignee can press the buttons for them too, for example for a supplier who's phoned in.

  • Start (while Pending Start) records that the work started now, and moves the job to In Progress.
  • Complete (while In Progress or Scheduled) asks for Completed At, the date and time the work was done in UK time (it can't be in the future or before the work started, so it can be filled in afterwards), and Completion Comments, what was done. The job moves to Completed.

6. Closing the job

Once a job is Completed, someone with Close jobs sees a Close button on its page. It closes the job today, and the job's Overview shows when and by whom.

  • Closing a planned job takes one more step: setting the schedule's next due date. MyFmHub suggests the date the work was completed plus the schedule's frequency. See Planned Jobs.
  • A closed job can be reopened with Reopen (with Reopen jobs). It goes back to Completed, and its close date and who closed it are cleared. Planned jobs can't be reopened, because closing them has already moved their schedule on.

7. Reassigning and adding helpers

  • Reassign (once a job is assigned, until it's closed) gives the job to a different supplier or person. Its status, dates and helpers stay as they are. The new assignee is told in the same way; the previous one isn't, so let them know yourself.
  • Add Helpers, next to the job's helpers on its Overview, adds more people to help. The next to a helper's name removes them. Helpers aren't told either way.

8. Follow-up work

If a job shows more work is needed, open its Follow Up Jobs tab and use Create Follow Up Job (with Jobs: Create), at any stage. The new job starts at the same location, which you can change. Saving it creates a New job with the source Follow Up, which goes through the usual steps.

The tab lists the job's follow-up jobs, and each follow-up job's Overview shows its Parent Job.

10. What's kept on record

  • The job's Overview shows who it's assigned to and the helpers, and when it was approved or rejected, assigned, started, completed (with the completion comments), and closed and by whom.
  • Who a job is assigned to is kept for audit, so a supplier or person with jobs assigned to them can't be deleted.

11. Importing jobs

Existing jobs, for example from another system, can be imported from an Excel file under Import data > Jobs. The import page lists every column, and its Excel template has dropdowns for the location, status, job category and assigned to. A few points:

  • Imported jobs are reactive jobs. Planned jobs come from planned job schedules, so they can't be imported.
  • status can be any job status, such as New, In Progress or Completed. Leave it blank for New.
  • assigned_to is the name of a supplier, or the full name of someone in People. Jobs that are Pending Acceptance through to Closed need one; New, Unassigned and Rejected jobs must leave it blank. When two people share a name, add the person's email in brackets, such as Sam Jones (sam@example.com), which is how the Excel template's dropdown shows people. If more than one supplier or person still matches, the row isn't imported: import it without the assignee (as Unassigned) and assign it on the job instead.
  • helpers is a list of people's full names separated by commas, such as Sam Jones, Alex Smith. Any of them can have the email in brackets in the same way. The person the job is assigned to can't also be a helper.
  • Nobody is told about imported jobs, and the dates the job's buttons normally record (approved, assigned, started, completed and closed) are left blank.
