Sites, Buildings and Zones

MyFmHub records where things are in three levels:

  • Site: a location you look after, such as an office, school or depot.
  • Building: a building at a site, such as Main Building or North Block.
  • Zone: an area of a building, such as Ground Floor, Plant Room or Roof.

Every asset, job and job schedule belongs to a site. You can also say which building it's in, and which zone of that building. Buildings and zones are optional, so you only need them where they help.

Adding buildings and zones

Buildings are added from a site: open the site, go to its Buildings tab and choose Add Building. Zones are added from a building in the same way: open the building, go to its Zones tab and choose Add Zone.

A building's page also has tabs listing the assets and job schedules in it.

Names

  • Each site in your organisation needs a different name.
  • Each building at a site needs a different name. Two sites can both have a "Main Building".
  • Each zone in a building needs a different name. Two buildings can both have a "Ground Floor".

Names are compared ignoring capitals, so "North Block" and "north block" count as the same name.

Renaming and moving

You can rename a building or zone at any time by editing it. A building can't be moved to a different site, and a zone can't be moved to a different building. This keeps everything in a building at the same site as the building. If a building was added to the wrong site, add it again at the right site and move its assets, jobs and job schedules across.

Choosing a location

When you create or edit an asset, job or job schedule, choose the Site first. The Building list then shows only the buildings at that site, and the Zone list only the zones in the chosen building. If you change the site, a building that isn't at the new site is cleared, and the same goes for the zone when you change the building.

Building and zone are both optional, but a zone can only be chosen with its building. MyFmHub checks the combination when you save, so an asset can never be in a building at a different site.

A job raised from a job schedule is given the schedule's site, building and zone.

Finding things by location

The Assets, Jobs and Job Schedules lists can be filtered by site, building and zone. These filters work the same way as the form: pick a site to narrow the buildings, then a building to narrow the zones. Downloads from those lists follow the filters, and include Building and Zone columns.

Deleting

  • Deleting a site deletes everything at it: its buildings and zones, and its assets, jobs and job schedules.
  • Deleting a building deletes its zones. Its assets, jobs and job schedules aren't deleted: they stay at the site, with no building or zone.
  • Deleting a zone doesn't delete anything in it: its assets, jobs and job schedules stay in the building, with no zone.

Importing

Sites, buildings and zones can all be imported from a CSV file under Import data. Import them in that order, because each one refers to the one before:

  1. Sites: the name must not already be used by another site.
  2. Buildings: a name and the name of an existing site.
  3. Zones: a name, plus the names of an existing site and of a building at that site.

Asset and job schedule imports have optional building and zone columns. Leave them blank for no building or zone. If you fill them in, the building must be at the row's site and the zone must be in that building, and a zone needs a building too. Rows that don't match aren't imported, and the import's results say why.

An import of buildings can't be undone once any of them have zones, assets, jobs or job schedules, and an import of zones can't be undone once any of them have assets, jobs or job schedules.

Permissions

Buildings and Zones have their own rows in the user group permissions, with view, create, edit and delete like the other records. A user group needs Sites: View to see a site's Buildings tab, and Buildings: Create to add buildings to it.
